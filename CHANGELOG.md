# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- Assign a pet to a specialization directly from the Pet Journal. Right-click a collected pet, choose **Apply to Spec**, then select a specialization.
- Localize settings, the Pet Journal menu, and chat messages for all Retail client languages.

## 1.1.0

- Assign titles by specialization under **Titles** in `/fluff`. Choose an earned title, **No Title** to hide it, or **No-Op** to keep your current title. Changes apply on your next keypress outside combat, and titles that are already active are skipped.
- Enable optional chat messages for queued and completed title changes or pet summons. Each settings page has its own controls, and logging is off by default.
- Manually dismissed pets stay dismissed across loading screens until you summon a pet, switch specializations, edit the active assignment, or reload.
- The companion pet settings page is now called **Pets** in English.

## 1.0.1

- Add an MIT license with the 2026 mandingavudu copyright notice.

## 1.0.0

Initial release of Specializations Fluff for World of Warcraft Retail.

- Assign a companion pet per character and specialization. The `/fluff` **Companions** picker shows 15 pets per page, puts starred favorites first, and clears the search after selection. Assignments apply on login, spec changes, and edits to the active assignment. The addon restores the last summoned pet after a despawn, remembers manually summoned pets across loading screens, and defers companion checks until combat ends.
